About the Faculty of Sciences

The Faculty of Sciences is part of the University of M'Hamed Bougara in Boumerdes, Algeria. It offers students the opportunity to learn, explore different scientific fields, and develop their knowledge and skills.

The faculty brings together different departments, each focusing on a specific field:

  • Computer Science
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Agronomy

The faculty aims to provide a good learning environment where students can gain knowledge, improve their skills, and prepare for their future careers. It also supports scientific research and encourages students to discover and learn new things.
