About the Faculty of Sciences

The University of M’Hamed Bougara (UMBB) is located in Boumerdes, Algeria, and offers students the opportunity to study a wide range of academic and scientific disciplines. It aims to provide quality education, promote scientific research, and help students gain the knowledge and skills needed for their future careers.

The Faculty of Sciences is an important part of the university and contributes to education and scientific development. It brings together several scientific disciplines, including Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Agronomy. Through its academic programs and learning environment, the faculty encourages students to explore new ideas, develop their skills, and participate in scientific discovery.
